Privacy policy

Data controller

Evolvix Global, S.L. is the controller responsible for processing the personal data provided through this site's forms and contact channels, in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 on the Protection of Personal Data and the guarantee of digital rights (LOPDGDD).

Purpose and legal basis

The data provided is processed to manage information requests, business proposals and enquiries addressed to any of the holding's divisions. The legal basis is the data subject's express consent when submitting their data through the enabled channels.

Data will be retained for as long as necessary to handle the request and, thereafter, for the legally required periods. Data will not be disclosed to third parties except where legally required.

Cookie policy

What are cookies?

Cookies are small files stored on the user's browser that allow their device to be recognized on subsequent visits, improving the browsing experience.

Cookies used on this site

This site uses only technical cookies that are strictly necessary for it to function correctly. No analytics, advertising or third-party tracking cookies are used without the user's prior consent.

Managing cookies

Users can allow, block or delete cookies installed on their device through their browser's settings. Please refer to your browser's documentation for more information.
