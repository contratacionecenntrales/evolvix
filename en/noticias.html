Group news and perspectives.

A look at how Evolvix Global's six divisions are evolving — trends, context, and how we approach each sector.

Sectors

What we are seeing in each division

01 — 06
01

Artificial Intelligence

AI is no longer an experiment — it's the edge that goes to whoever integrates it well.

More companies are competing with processes that think for themselves: automated decisions, demand forecasting, early anomaly detection. The difference is no longer having AI, but having it properly integrated into real operations. At Evolvix Global we design that integration from day one, not as an isolated pilot.

View division
02

International Trade

Supply chains are being reshuffled — stand still and you lose margin.

Tariffs, new logistics routes and suppliers relocating are forcing every cross-border operation to be rethought. Companies that structure their international trade well turn that complexity into a cost and speed advantage. That's exactly where we work with our clients, operation by operation.

View division
03

Business Intelligence

Deciding by gut feeling no longer competes with deciding by data.

Leadership teams that check a live dashboard make faster decisions with less margin for error than those waiting for the monthly report. Turning scattered data into clear decisions is increasingly the real competitive edge — and it's the daily work of our business intelligence division.

View division
04

Cybersecurity

The cost of an incident is no longer measured only in money — it's measured in lost trust.

Attacks no longer just aim to steal data; they aim to disrupt operations for as long as possible. Static perimeter defense is no longer enough against threats that evolve weekly. Continuous auditing and early response are today’s difference between a contained incident and a public crisis.

View division
05

Digital Infrastructure & Cloud

The cloud stopped being a one-off migration — it's now an ongoing architecture decision.

Companies that scale without friction are the ones that designed their infrastructure to grow, not just to work today. Well-planned cloud-native architectures cut operating costs and prevent the outages that cost reputation. It’s the standard we build every infrastructure project to.

View division
06

Legal, Compliance & Structuring

International regulation is no longer paperwork — it's part of the business design.

Operating across several countries means complying with frameworks that shift from jurisdiction to jurisdiction, and a corporate-structuring mistake can cost far more than the advice would have. Getting compliance right from the start prevents future roadblocks and opens doors a poorly planned structure closes.
